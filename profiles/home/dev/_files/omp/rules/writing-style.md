# Writing style

## Characters

- Do not use characters that can't be typed on a keyboard. For arrows, prefer combined symbols such as `->`.
- Do not use em dashes (—), en dashes (–), or ellipses (…) in your responses.
- Replace em dashes and ellipses with commas, full stops, or simple hyphens.
- Use simple, clean, and direct punctuation suitable for informal human writing.
